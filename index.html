Questions people ask

Still unsure? Write to us at hello@yourdomain.com

What counts as checking in?

Any of these resets your timer to 90 days: clicking I'm still here in a reminder email, pressing it on your will page, or saving a change to your will.

What if I miss the reminders?

We send two, 15 days and 1 day before the deadline. If neither gets a reply, your will is sent on day 90. Add our address to your contacts so reminders don't land in spam. If you'll be offline for a long time, check in just before you go and you'll have 90 days from that moment.

Is this a legal will?

No. Amanah delivers your words by email. In most countries a legal will has to be written, signed and witnessed under local law. Use Amanah alongside a registered will, for example to tell your family where that will is kept.

I'm Muslim. Does Amanah follow the rules of wasiyyah?

Amanah delivers what you write. It doesn't divide an estate. In Islamic law a bequest is generally limited to one-third of what remains after debts, and the schools of law differ on bequests to heirs. Ask a scholar you trust before you write down shares.

Who can read my will?

Your will is stored until it's delivered or you delete it. The people you named see it only after delivery, and each sees only their own private note. The service operator can technically access stored data, so don't write passwords, PINs or card numbers.

I lost the email with my link.

Go to My will and use Email me my link. We'll send it to the address you confirmed.

Does it work in my country and language?

Yes. All you need is an email address. Write in any language, including Urdu and Arabic. Dates are shown in your own time zone.

Privacy

What we store. Your name and email, your will, the names, emails and notes of the people you chose, your time zone, and the dates you checked in.

Why. To send you reminders and, if you stop checking in, to deliver your will.

Who sees it. The people you named, only after delivery. Each person sees the main message and only their own note. The service operator can technically access stored data, which is why we ask you not to include passwords or financial details.

Deleting. Deleting your will wipes its text and the list of people. Drafts whose email is never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.

Email. Emails are sent through Google's mail service.

Terms

Not a legal will. Amanah delivers a message by email. It doesn't replace a will made under the law of your country.

Delivery isn't guaranteed. Email can be blocked, filtered as spam or sent to an inbox nobody reads. Keep a written will and tell someone you trust where it is.

Your responsibility. Only write what you have the right to share. Don't use Amanah to send unwanted or harmful messages. We may remove wills that do.

Checking in. If you don't respond for 90 days, your will is sent. We can't recall an email once it has been delivered.
